The combined model estimates speech, music (including singing), and sound
effects. The application retains music plus sound effects for the optional
"Keep singing; remove dialogue" backing mode. Inference is probabilistic:
speech remnants or unintended changes to singing, music, and effects are possible.

Inference source: https://github.com/kwatcharasupat/bandit-v2

The inference-only source replaces the training framework base with
torch.nn.Module. The application supplies local CPU execution, sequential
mono-model inference for each stereo channel, full-duration streaming
overlap-add, and one global clipping-safety gain. The learned architecture
and checkpoint weights are not replaced, converted, or quantized.

Model download and repair require explicit permission. Verified cached
weights are reused offline. Audio and inference remain local; this feature
does not upload media. Weights and private example media are not distributed
inside the application ZIP.
