Preprocessing, Unicode token grouping and DTW word-boundary calculations in
caption_timing_worker.py adapt the corresponding faster-whisper v1.2.1 helpers:
https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper/tree/v1.2.1/faster_whisper
Modifications: bounded disk windows, explicit token coverage validation,
timestamp-envelope constraints and conservative uncertain-word probabilities.
The faster-whisper package and its media decoder are not shipped or used.
